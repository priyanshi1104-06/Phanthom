Privacy

Last updated 23 October 2026.

Your code

Phantom's engine and its sandboxes run on your own computer. Your code is not uploaded to Phantom. If you turn on Phantom's AI features, the code being checked is sent to the AI model provider to suggest inputs and read what a function is meant to do. You can use Phantom with the AI off.

Your account

When you sign in with Google, Phantom receives your name and email address from Google, and nothing else: not your password, contacts or files. They are stored in Amazon Cognito, in Amazon Web Services' US East (N. Virginia) region, and used to run your account and early access, and to email you about Phantom. Phantom does not sell them or share them for advertising.

This website

The website uses no cookies, no analytics and no trackers. Fonts and images are served from the site itself. Signing in keeps a sign-in token in your browser tab until you close it or sign out.
